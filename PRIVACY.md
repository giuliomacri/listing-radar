# Listing Radar: privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Listing Radar is an unofficial browser extension that alerts you to new second-hand listings matching searches you save. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vinted.

**What it stores.** Your saved searches, settings, the list of recent finds and (only if you turn on Telegram forwarding) your Telegram bot token and chat id. All of this is kept in your browser's local extension storage. It is never sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking.

**What it connects to.**
- The marketplace site you add a search for (for example vinted.it), from your own browser, to read the public search results for your saved searches. It does not log in to your account.
- The marketplace's image servers (vinted.net), to show listing photos in notifications.
- Telegram's API (api.telegram.org), only if you enable Telegram forwarding. Listing details (title, price, photo, link) are then sent to the chat you chose, using your own bot.

**Permissions.** Storage (keep your settings), alarms (run checks on a schedule), notifications (show alerts), and optional site access that you grant per site when you add a search.

**Your control.** Removing a search deletes its data. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.
